Breakfast platter

Ever wondered hot to make a breakfast taste good without needing any different ingredients than what you are probably already using?

Well worry not here I present my gourmet got-to breakfast which I have been making almost everyday since the start of the year! Once you make it, you won't want to have anything else for breakfast!

Description

This would sound like a typical normall breakfast consisting of toast, eggs and bacon but the difference that makes this unique is in how it is prepared. The eggs are inspired from Gordon Ramsay's creamy scrambled eggs which are put on toast along with bacon. There is your breakfast sandwhich

Ingredients(1 serving)

  • 3 eggs
  • 2 slices of toast
  • 2 slices of bacon or any other ham
  • 1 tsp of fresh cream
  • two knobs of butter
  • honey
  • salt
  • pepper
  • olive oil

Steps

  1. Crack 3 eggs in a cold small saucepan and sprinkle it with salt to taste. I eyeball the amount but so far two generous sprinkles by hand make my eggs perfectly salted.
  2. heat the tove to medium-high, but more on the medium side.
  3. take a rubber spatula and put the saucepan on the stove to heat.
  4. Whis the eggs, butter and salt with the spatula and once heated make sure to keep whisking to stop the eggs from cooking and settling on the bottom of the pan.
  5. Scrape the curdles and keep doing this till it starts to curdle too much at once, then take it of the heat but keep whicking and scraping!
  6. Once eggs are of creamy consistency or a bit dry, add the other knob of butter, the cream and pepper to taste. Turn off the heat, the eggs are done.
  7. Toast the bread or buns to whatever your desire.
  8. Spread eggs on on your bread evenly
  9. If remaining, put eggs on plate as side dish and spread or apply honey on other bread
  10. Serve and enjoy!
